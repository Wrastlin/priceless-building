# PRODUCT.md — Price-Less Building Center

register: brand (storefront) / product (admin under /admin)

## What this is

The website for Price-Less Building Center, a family-run discount building
materials store at 825 Washington Street, Wausau, WI. Same building since 1978.
New liquidation stock arrives every Wednesday: doors, windows, cabinets,
vanities, hardware, trim. Two sister operations share the site: Builders Corner
(custom cabinetry and design) and 4 Squared (installation crew, Josh and Ty).

The site is a browsing catalog and trust builder, not e-commerce checkout.
People find an item online, then drive in. The admin is the real daily tool:
floor staff capture inventory with phone cameras and sticker tags, print
labels, manage aging stock.

## Users

- Wausau-area homeowners mid-project hunting a deal on a door or vanity.
- Contractors checking stock before driving over.
- Floor staff (admin): fast capture, dense tables, big touch targets.

## Owner and voice

Josh Nickel owns the store. He wants the site to feel like
remodelingjourney.com: warm, established, photo-led, trust-heavy
(Google stars, credentials, local roots), elegant serif headlines, navy and
gold. NOT a verbatim copy; our own version of that warmth.

Feature Josh and Ty. Never mention Jamus. The install brand is written
"4 Squared".

Brand voice words: sturdy, hand-painted, family-owned. The physical object is
a classic American lumber-yard sign: Clarendon letterforms, deep navy, brass
gold, warm cream.

## Strategic principles

- Premium-showroom warmth, but never lose the deal energy: this IS a discount
  store. Red is reserved exclusively for savings ("% off", was/now prices).
- Trust ladder on every marketing page: real reviews, real photos, real hours,
  the 1978 heritage.
- Photos of the actual store, mural, and inventory beat stock photography.
- Mobile first: most shoppers arrive from Facebook on phones.

## Anti-references

- The old industrial red/black big-box-catalog look (what we redesigned away).
- Generic Tailwind SaaS templates, hero-metric cards, icon-card grids.
- Verbatim remodelingjourney.com (Josefin Slab, Montserrat, #022646, #CF9725
  are all off-limits; the feel is borrowed, the system is ours).
