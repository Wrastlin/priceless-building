# AI-Generated / Synthetic Media Index

## Non-negotiable policy

Do NOT use AI images in brand/category/long-form motion ads unless Aaron explicitly approves a specific file. Prefer real photos always.

Every path below is **AI-GENERATED** or a synthetic staging plate. It must never be represented as a real Price-Less Building Center photo, real inventory photo, customer installation, store photo, or staff photography.

## Audit result

- `public/catalog-images/`: 93 files, Gemini-generated catalog heroes and residential scene variants. Source evidence: `scripts/gen-catalog-bulk.py`.
- `public/test-images/`: 19 files, Gemini-generated catalog tests and gap fills. Source evidence: `scripts/gen-test-images.py` and `scripts/gen-gap-images.py`.
- `public/staging/`: 4 files, synthetic staging-ground plates confirmed by visual spot-check.
- `public/sandbox/frames/`: excluded from this index because it contains 661 extracted frames, not scene variants or Gemini outputs.
- The live Gemini marketing-variant route and scene prompt builder generate ephemeral output and do not persist files under `public/`; any output saved later must be added to this index before use.

## Allowed use codes

- `fallback_only`: Product/listing hero fallback only. It is not real inventory proof and is excluded from brand/category/long-form motion ads by the policy above.
- `exclude_from_brand_ads`: Internal reference or compositing only. Do not publish in brand/category/long-form motion ads.
