# Real Photo Marketing Audit

Visual audit completed July 22, 2026. Scope was limited to real warehouse, product, project, storefront, mural, and logo assets. Team portraits and hours flyers were excluded.

## Summary

- Inspected: **55 images**
- Hero-grade: **13**
- Usable as plate or detail: **33**
- Reject or skip: **9**
- AI-generated: **0 detected**
- Heavily filtered or collage: **4 flagged**
- Not counted: `mural-wide.webp` could not be decoded by the image reader. Its existing manifest description was not treated as visual evidence.

Interpretation:

- **Hero** means strong enough to lead a page or motion sequence at its native practical size.
- **Plate** means useful supporting coverage, usually an aisle, room, storefront, or installation.
- **Detail** means a tight product or material insert.
- **Skip** means do not spend marketing space on it. A factual archive use may still exist.
- Legacy Google thumbnails are judged at their actual delivered resolution. Attractive subject matter does not rescue a fuzzy 223 px source.

## Duplicate and near-duplicate notes

- `builders-corner-hero.jpg` and `foursquared/kitchen-dark-shaker-marble-island.jpg` are exact visual duplicates. Do not show both in a cross-brand montage or claim they are separate projects.
- `foursquared/pergola-string-lights-night.jpg` and `foursquared/pergola-string-lights-night-2.jpg` are near-identical. Prefer `-2` when the structure matters; prefer the first only when a darker mood is intentional.
- `foursquared/pergola-patio-daylight.jpg`, `pergola-patio-wide.jpg`, and `pergola-patio-angle.jpg` are the same pergola project. Use one wide frame plus one detail at most.
- `foursquared/kitchen-white-island-shiplap.jpg` and `foursquared/kitchen-white-open-wood-accents.jpg` are the same kitchen. Prefer the open view as the lead and the shiplap elevation as support.
- `foursquared/kitchen-oak-mosaic-backsplash.jpg` and `foursquared/kitchen-counter-marble-detail.jpg` are the same kitchen. The countertop frame is the more useful marketing detail.
- July 2026 `floor-*` images should replace legacy warehouse thumbnails wherever subjects overlap. The largest quality gains are doors, trim, vanities, windows, lighting and general aisle coverage.

## AI, filtering and collage flags

- No inspected image showed AI-generation artifacts.
- `storefront-signage.webp` is a deliberate polaroid-style collage.
- `mural-detail.webp` is a two-part social collage rather than a clean photo.
- The daylight pergola set has a conspicuous warm, high-contrast social filter: `pergola-patio-daylight.jpg`, `pergola-patio-angle.jpg`, and `pergola-patio-wide.jpg`.
- The pergola night shots use heavy low-light processing and should not be treated as natural-color documentation.

## Practical selection guidance

- For Price-Less warehouse motion, start with the July door aisle, vanity row, butcher-block racks, window aisle and lumber room.
- Keep `dark-base-cabinets-warehouse-row.jpg` because its shallow depth and black cabinetry still provide a distinctive premium surplus shot.
- For finished-room credibility, lead with the dark shaker kitchen, the white marble kitchen and the Four Squared white-and-wood kitchen.
- Keep legacy installs small. None of the inspected legacy files should be enlarged into a desktop hero.
- The place/brand photography is the weakest bucket. A clean landscape storefront and a high-resolution straight-on mural frame remain priority capture gaps.
