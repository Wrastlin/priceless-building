## What motion graphics are for (Aaron)

1. **Eliminate words**, or make the rare word so bold it *is* the graphic.
2. **Nobody wants to read text over an image.** Type-on-photo is a last resort, not the default.
3. Either:
   - the **photo / product motion tells the story**, or
   - the **type itself is the motion graphic** (on a clean field — ink / cream void — not stamped on aisle photos).
4. If the picture already says it, **do not say it again.**  
   Example of failure: warehouse doors with the word **DOORS** on top. The viewer already knows what a door is.

## Commercial job

| Job | Not the job |
|-----|-------------|
| Grab attention in a scroll | Explain every department |
| Signal: *thousands of items / we have a lot* | Inventory catalog narration |
| Signal: *this place is cool / they know design* | Soft corporate slideshow |
| Make people want to come in | Teach product names on every beat |

End feeling: **“Oh my gosh — they have everything.”** Shown, not narrated.

## Structure that fits this intent

Preferred spine (words nearly absent):

1. **Place punch** — mural / seal energy. Almost no type.
2. **Install cake (Josh)** — finished remodeled kitchens / baths / outdoor. Longer holds. This is the emotional peak — great installs, great brands, craft.
3. **Store volume** — rapid floor/warehouse plates. **Silent.** Thousands of items.
4. **Hero MG moment (optional)** — product select → lift → slam into Josh’s finished rooms.
5. **Category collage (optional)** — one mosaic beat for “we have it all.”
6. **Close** — void punch (FOR LESS / quality discount) + brand seal + address. Minimal.

## Ban list (hard)

- Labeling a photo with its obvious noun (`DOORS` over doors, `WINDOWS` over windows, `CABINETS` over cabinets)
- Essay stacks over lifestyle/warehouse plates
- “Ready to discover…” style tour-guide copy
- Vibe-coded UI: cards, pills, badges, button boxes, multi-line info modules
- Desktop-first 16:9 as the master — **9:16 Shorts is the master** for posting
- Treating a Ken Burns photo montage as “motion graphics”

## Allow list

- Isolated product on cream/ink void (Kohler / Apple grammar)
- Select → lift → slam transforms
- Hard cuts, snappy scale, collage / mosaic reveals
- One punch word on a **void** (e.g. FOR LESS) — type *is* the graphic
- End collage of many categories for “we have it all”
- Real photos from the full library (hundreds) — reconsider curation every pass

## Craft bar

- [Buff Motion Showreel 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gQldOO6KiU8) — punch, product as hero, stand out
- Kohler Shorts — centered type on void / product field; **not** type stamped on busy plates
- Peer: Apple product films, Brizo / Signature Hardware / GROHE Shorts energy

## Format

- **Master:** 1080×1920 @ 30fps (mobile / Reels / Shorts / TikTok)
- Length target: ~20–40s (shorter if punchier)
- 16:9 is a secondary crop only after the vertical cut works
