## Camera / motion rules

**Allowed:** Hero Cam, Slow Dolly In, shallow Arc L/R, Focus Change, Whip Pan between warehouse reality and graphic cards, Lazy Susan only on small hardware later.

**Banned for this cut:** Bullet Time, FPV, Dutch Angle, extreme Crash Zoom, fake installed-room as “proof,” AI humans.

## Higgsfield split

1. **Vibe Motion** — all typography, measurement line, category labels, end card, wipes.
2. **Cinema image-to-video** — 1–2 short plates from aisle + craftsman stills (3–5s each), then composited into the Vibe Motion timeline conceptually (or sequenced in edit).
3. **Hook predictor** — score three openings; keep the winner.

### Hook variants to score

- A: `BEFORE YOU BUY A DOOR…`
- B: `HUNDREDS OF DOORS. WAUSAU.`
- C: `STOP PAYING BIG-BOX DOOR PRICES`

## Success criteria

- Feels like a brand film, not a generated product demo
- Warehouse reads as real (concrete, racks, labels, unfinished wood)
- Savings language is honest at category level
- Works muted with captions (type-led)
- Reusable: swap stills next quarter without rebuilding the motion language
