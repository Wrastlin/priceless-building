## Corrected source distillation

The source is not a ten-second hero hold. It has nine readable beats, including seven distinct product crops after the opening room view. The first shot is split into two editorial beats because the brand lockup changes the frame's purpose.

## Locked production rules

- Montserrat/Gotham 300 lead-in and 700 brand, all caps, with line 2 indented.
- Product motion stays within a 5% scale change.
- Isolated product only. No aisle pans and no AI people.
- The Price-Less twin uses no numeric price until the amount is verified.
- Remotion owns timing, type, crops, color fields, and final assembly.
- Higgsfield may later supply one approved product plate per scene, never one plate held across the entire Short.
