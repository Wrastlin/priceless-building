## 1. WHO WE ARE

**Price-Less Building Center** is the warehouse. Discount and surplus building materials in central Wisconsin since 1978, started by Don Midlikowski as a weekend operation selling cancelled-order and surplus stock, full-time by 1982. Doors, windows, cabinets, vanities, countertops, lighting, hardware, and trim at warehouse prices, including legit liquidation finds from brands you know (JELD-WEN, Andersen, Kohler, Duravit are visible on the actual floor). The voice is scale, surplus, real savings, inventory that changes constantly. Site hero: "The whole home, priced for less."

**Builders Corner Cabinetry & Design** is the premium side. Custom kitchen and bath cabinets designed and built in its own Wausau shop since 1983, with a showroom at the same address where customers pick real samples. Process on the site: Consult, Design, Build, Install. The voice is premier finished work and quality brands you trust: parchment, brass, showroom calm.

**4 Squared** is the install crew under the same roof, led by Josh with Ty. Kitchens, baths, basements, doors, finish carpentry, and whole-house remodels across central Wisconsin (Wausau, Schofield, Weston, Rib Mountain, Rothschild, Mosinee). Two-year labor warranty. Same crew lead from demo through final walkthrough. Feature Josh and Ty only; never feature Jamus.

**How they interlock:** three brands, one building at 825 Washington St. Josh Nickel has run all three since 2019. The one-line story arc, straight from the homepage: "From the warehouse floor to a finished kitchen." Walk in for a door, leave with a designer's number if you want one. Locked creative framing: hip local Wausau one-stop, premier remodel plus discount supplies, all under one roof.

## 4. ASSET GAPS

- **Place identity hi-res:** no clean landscape storefront and no straight-on high-res mural capture (named capture gap in `media/README.md`); `storefront-sign-on-brick.webp` is low res. No exterior drone or downtown-context establishing shot.
- **People:** zero usable install-in-progress or crew-at-work b-roll; Josh/Ty portraits exist but are on the standing exclude list. Need an approved on-camera shoot of Josh and Ty (never Jamus) for angle 4.
- **Motion coverage:** only one unscripted phone walkthrough (2026-06-11); no stabilized aisle moves, no product turntables, no macro finish passes for Builders Corner styles 03/04.
- **Category thinness:** windows (9 photos, mostly racks), hardware, trim, and paint are shallow; no strong countertop-slab hero; legacy finished rooms are tiny Google thumbnails (about 223px, cannot go full-bleed).
- **Freshness proof:** no truck unload, forklift, dock, or restock footage to sell "inventory changes constantly."
- **Voice:** no recorded owner/customer audio; testimonial claims currently text-only (4.8 Google is citable).
- **Product masters:** wood-look exterior door still shrink-wrapped in its master; crystal-ceiling-fan chandelier needs a clean re-mask; motion-only masters (dragon basin, iron-studded pair, espresso pair, black-faucet vanity) must never run as SKU ads.
